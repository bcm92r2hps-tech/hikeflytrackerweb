3Purposes

  • Record your outings and automatically create flying site sheets.
  • Share site sheets between users (Planner tab).
  • Manage your account and keep the service secure.
  • Moderate the Planner (reports, blocks, sheet removal).
  • Show maps and an outing's weather, and suggest a name for a new site.
  • Send you app news, only if you asked for it.

4Publication of site sheets

When you are signed in to the Planner, each site in your logbook is published automatically, with no confirmation step, and is visible to all app users. Sheets less than 100 m apart are combined on screen into a single take-off. In Supabase, each sheet is linked to its author's account and the developer can see that address for moderation, but your name and email address are never shown to other users.

You can report a sheet or block its author: you then no longer see their sheets, and you can unblock them in Settings. The author is not notified. The developer can remove a sheet, for example if its content is illegal: it is then visible only to its author, with the reason for removal.

5Private zones

You can set private zones in Settings › Private zones (« Zones privées »), for example around your home. Within 200 m of these places, the points of your ascent paths are not published; they remain complete in your logbook. Your zones are stored on your device and in your logbook backups, not on our servers. Address search uses Apple's map service.

No zone is created automatically: set one before signing in to the Planner if you sometimes start from home. A private zone does not hide a take-off position, landing places or ascent figures (elevation gain, distance). The visible start of a track may still suggest a location, especially an isolated one. Sheets are updated when you change your zones, but a track already published may have been viewed or exported beforehand.

6App news

If you tick "Recevoir les nouvelles de HFT" ("Receive HFT news") in Settings, we use your email address to occasionally send you information about the app (new features, updates). This is optional and unticked by default: declining does not affect your use of the app. The legal basis is your consent; we keep your choice and its date so that we can demonstrate it.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by unticking the box, by using the unsubscribe link in every email, or by writing to hikeflytracker@gmail.com. Withdrawal does not affect emails already sent.

7Processors and third-party services

Planner and account data are hosted by Supabase, acting as a processor on behalf of HFT under its Data Processing Addendum (DPA).

  • Hosting: European region in Supabase.
  • Security: AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS in transit; Supabase is SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO 27001 certified.
  • Supabase may only use this data according to HFT's instructions and may not sell it.
  • Should data be accessed from a country outside Switzerland or the EU, this transfer is covered by the standard contractual clauses in Supabase's DPA.

Reports (text, the sheet concerned and the reporter's email address, so they can be answered) are also sent by email to hikeflytracker@gmail.com, a Gmail address provided by Google. These emails are kept by Google under its own privacy policy and may be stored outside Switzerland and the EU.

Sign-in emails, reports and, if you signed up, app news are sent by Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), our email provider. Your address is not sold or used for any other purpose.

Some features directly query third-party services, which receive coordinates and your IP address: Open-Meteo (an outing's weather, a landing field's altitude), OpenStreetMap via Overpass and Nominatim (names suggested for a new site), OpenTopoMap (map backgrounds) and Apple (Maps, address search). No account is created with them and they do not receive your email address.

HFT uses no third-party advertising or analytics tools.

8Retention

  • Account data: as long as your account exists; deleted within 30 days after account deletion.
  • Reports: the reporter's email address and the related emails are deleted once the report has been handled.
  • News: your choice for as long as your account exists; your address stays on the mailing list until you unsubscribe or delete your account. After you unsubscribe using an email link, Brevo may keep it on a suppression list, solely to ensure we no longer write to you.
  • Technical logs: for Supabase's limited retention period.
  • Data on your device: until you delete it or uninstall the app.

When you delete your account, your email address is erased. The site sheets you published remain in the Planner, including their ascent tracks, landings and notes, but they are no longer linked to your account and you can no longer edit them. To remove your sheets, delete your sites in the app before deleting your account, or write to hikeflytracker@gmail.com. Reports you have sent are kept, without any link to your account.

9Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the GDPR, you may request access to, correction, deletion or portability of your data, and object to its processing. You can delete your account directly in the app or by writing to hikeflytracker@gmail.com.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or the data protection authority in your country.

10Children

HFT is not intended for children under 16 and does not knowingly collect their data.

11Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version; for significant changes, the app will let you know.
